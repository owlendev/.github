# Owlendev 🦉
### A clearer way to build software.

Owlendev is a software development company founded by Omar and Leen.

We design and build custom software, business platforms, and digital products that help businesses operate more efficiently and manage their own systems with confidence.

Our goal is simple: turn ideas into reliable, practical software without adding unnecessary complexity.

## What We Do
- Custom Software Development — Complete web applications tailored to business needs.
- Business Platforms — Booking systems, management dashboards, and operational tools.
- Full-Stack Development — Frontend, backend, databases, and API integrations.
- Payments & Integrations — Connecting applications with payment providers and external services.
- Cloud Deployment — Deploying applications, configuring domains, and managing hosting.
- SaaS Products — Developing reusable software products that can serve multiple businesses.

## Our Approach
We believe business owners should be able to manage their own platforms without depending on developers for every small change.

We build configurable, maintainable software with a focus on usability, practical architecture, and long-term value.

Our work includes both custom solutions developed for individual clients and reusable products that can serve multiple customers.

## Our Technology

We choose technologies based on the needs of each project. Our development work includes technologies such as:

- Go
- React
- SQL databases
- REST APIs
- Cloud infrastructure
- Payment and third-party integrations
- Artificial Intelligence (LLMs)

Our technology choices may evolve as our products and projects grow.
